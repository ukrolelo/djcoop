About Email Accounts

To use the mail client, you need to configure your POP3 and SMTP settings for your email provider. Here are some common settings:

Provider POP3 Server POP3 Port SMTP Server SMTP Port SSL
Gmail pop.gmail.com 995 smtp.gmail.com 587 Yes
Outlook/Hotmail outlook.office365.com 995 smtp.office365.com 587 Yes
Yahoo pop.mail.yahoo.com 995 smtp.mail.yahoo.com 587 Yes
Important Notes

For Gmail and some other providers, you may need to enable POP3 access in your account settings and use an app password instead of your regular password.
